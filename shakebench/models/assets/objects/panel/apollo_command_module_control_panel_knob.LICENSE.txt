Use in ShakeBench
--------------
The STL triangle topology is retained unchanged. At runtime ShakeBench converts
millimetres to metres, maps the STL axes into the panel's tangent/lateral/
normal frame, translates the base onto the mounting bezel, and assigns a
neutral display color. The physical collision proxy remains a separate simple
cylinder and is not derived from the licensed visual mesh.
