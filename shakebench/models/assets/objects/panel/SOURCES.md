# Control panel assets

## Console, button and indicators

The inclined console layout, housing, bezels, fasteners, indicator lamps and
button are adapted from the MIT-licensed
[wyl03291211/ShakeBench](https://github.com/wyl03291211/ShakeBench) demo, commit
`22f526a7e73cea8e0b9f8c09a597602484a814a0`. Its code notice is preserved in
`DEMO_CODE_LICENSE.txt`. The compact console, printed faceplate labels and
materials are authored in `panel.xml` and `panel_faceplate_print.png`.

## Independently constructed controls: `original_controls`

The rotary grip, dark shoulder, dial, inset, toggle nut, shoulder and lever are original analytic geometry generated by `tools/generate_panel_controls.py`. The single `tools/generate_panel_controls.py` entry point regenerates the flat handle, directional paint, upper contact hulls and knob inertia while preserving unchanged analytic control resources. Explicit sampled plan contours, planar caps, rounded crowns, radial surfaces and fitted convex hulls define the meshes. No vendor CAD, Apollo/Interruptor vertices or topology are used or packaged. The controls carry no vendor logos.

Official IDEC HW1S-3TP11 and Schneider XB5AD33 product photographs were inspected only as manufacturing-detail references. The final waist-shaped flat grip and one-ended direction band are independently parameterized from a product-photo silhouette and user revisions, not a transformed vendor mesh. Existing finishes are artistic choices, not measured material data.

- https://apem.asset.akeneo.cloud/idec_product_images/media/HW1S3TP11_listing_image.jpg
- https://download.schneider-electric.com/files?p_Doc_Ref=XB5_633_CPMFS17192&p_File_Type=rendition_1500_jpg

The gray flat grip is 27.511588 mm long, at most 8.705727 mm wide and 6.800 mm wide at its waist. Both long sides and both end faces have 8.925 mm of exposed straight height above the 14.95 mm shoulder datum; the crown reaches 9.4775 mm above it. This applies the user-requested 15 percent height reduction to the original plate preview. A 0.5525 mm crown radius joins the vertical faces to the planar top. The 2.2 mm black indicator occupies only the positive tangent half and wraps onto that end face. It rotates with the control toward existing OFF/ON labels. Physical handle-only grasp and wrist-path acceptance are pending; no previous capture is relabelled as validation of this geometry.

All display meshes have zero mass and `contype=conaffinity=0`. `tools/fit_panel_collisions.py` derives 38 compound knob contacts, the lever and fixed collar/shoulder contacts solely from these local visual meshes. Explicit inertia uses volume-normalized geometric estimates while preserving knob/lever masses; official joint parameters, controller and success thresholds are retained. Results are not contact-equivalent to older panel revisions, and historical demonstrations do not automatically acquire this appearance.

Regenerate from the repository root with:

```bash
python tools/generate_panel_controls.py
```
