===================================

No license, author, attribution, or redistribution terms were present in the

Geometry selection
------------------

Retained FBX components:
- Sphere.001: moving toggle handle.
- Bolt.001: threaded mounting body and shallow hexagonal collar.

Excluded components include Cube.002 and Cube.003 (the large rectangular
enclosure below the collar), plus unrelated screws, dial plate, text, indicator
planes, camera, and lights.  The handle's single 32-edge opening was capped in
its hidden overlap with the mounting body before topology-preserving decimation.

Generated runtime files:
- switch_control_base.runtime.stl: 1,800 triangles, closed/watertight.
- switch_control_handle.runtime.stl: 500 triangles, closed/watertight.
- switch_control.runtime.json: selection, topology, bounds, scaling, and sampled
  surface-distance metrics.
